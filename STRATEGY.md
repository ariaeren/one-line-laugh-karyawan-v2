# Strategy

Entry: **My phone battery and my motivation both hit 1% in the same meeting.**

- **One sentence, ≤200 characters:** 67 characters including the final period, with no line breaks or extra whitespace. A single independent clause with a coordinated subject, so there is exactly one sentence.
- **Twist at the end:** the setup reads like an ordinary phone complaint; the last word, "meeting," reveals that the real shortage is human, not electrical. The punch line lands on the final noun.
- **Everyday truth:** dead batteries and low-energy meetings are instantly familiar to anyone with a phone and a calendar; the joke works because the comparison feels uncomfortably accurate.
- **Economy of words:** no setup paragraph, no explanation, no adjectives doing decorative work. One verb ("hit"), one number ("1%"), and one shared noun frame both halves, so every word carries the twist.
- **Freshness:** the specific 1% parallel avoids generic "I'm tired" humor; the deadpan, literal tone makes the self-deprecation feel new rather than rehearsed.
